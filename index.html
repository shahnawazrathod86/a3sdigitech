Aarogya Connect

Your Health. Your Services. Your Connect.

A citizen health companion for India. Find hospitals, PHCs, CHCs, pharmacies, blood banks and AB-PMJAY empanelled hospitals near you, keep health reminders, and track readings like BP, glucose and BMI. My Family Health keeps a private profile, medicine reminders and an encrypted document vault for each family member.

  • English, Hindi and Gujarati
  • Health records stay on your phone
  • General information only, not medical advice

PaisaSet

Har Kharch Ka Hisaab, Har Mahine Bachat.

An offline household budget and expense tracker for Indian families. A Safe Daily Spend view shows how much you can spend each day to last the month, with Cash vs UPI breakdowns, PDF statements and Excel/CSV exports made on the phone.

  • 100% offline, no internet permission
  • English, Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati
