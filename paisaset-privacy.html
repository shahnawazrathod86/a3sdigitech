PaisaSet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026
App: PaisaSet (Ghar Ka Budget), Android, package com.a3sdigitech.ghar_ka_budget
Publisher and data controller: A3S DIGITECH, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India
Contact: support@a3sdigitech.com

PaisaSet is a household budget and expense tracker. Your money records stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google AdMob needs to show ads (section 5), and anything you choose to export or share yourself (section 4).

1. What we store on your device

Everything you type is stored only in a local database inside the app’s private storage on your phone: income and expense entries (amount, date, category, payment method, optional note), monthly and category budgets, recurring bills, savings goals and deposits, and your settings (language, theme, whether App Lock is on). A3S DIGITECH does not run any server for this data, never receives it and cannot see it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it if your phone is lost, reset or the app is uninstalled, unless you saved a backup file yourself (section 4).

Notes may contain personal details. Enter only what you need. The database is stored as ordinary (not encrypted) data in the app’s protected folder; Android stops other apps from reading it, but anyone with access to an unlocked or rooted phone could. Keep your phone secured and use App Lock if you share it.

2. What we do not collect or do

  • No account, sign-in, name, phone number or email address is needed or collected.
  • We do not connect to your bank, cards or UPI, read SMS or notifications, read your contacts, or access your location, camera, microphone, photos or files (other than the file you pick or save yourself when you export or restore).
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs of our own, and we do not sell your data.
  • The app contains no AI feature, and none of your data is used to train any AI model.

4. Exports, backups and sharing

You can save a monthly PDF statement, a CSV spreadsheet and a JSON backup, and restore from a backup file. These files are created on your phone and saved or shared only where you choose (for example Files, email, WhatsApp or a printer). Once a file leaves the app, the app or service you chose handles it under its own policy. Backup files are plain, readable text with a checksum that detects accidental damage; they are not encrypted, so store them carefully. Spreadsheet cells are protected against formula injection. Before one of these actions the app may show a full-screen advertisement (section 5).

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PaisaSet is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner at the bottom of the Home and Insights screens, and, before you export, back up or restore, a full-screen ad that may be a video, shown at most twice per app session. Ads never appear in the entry forms, on the Transactions or Settings screens, or on the lock screen.

To select, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google can collect and process your device’s advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information (model, operating system version, language), diagnostics and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising policies. We do not receive this data.

Your financial data is never shared with AdMob or any advertiser. No entry, note, budget, bill or goal is ever passed to the ad SDK or an ad request.

Consent and choices. Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google’s consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it later under Settings, Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads (or Google Settings, Ads). Ads are not designed for or directed at children (section 8).

7. Your control over your data, and deleting it

  • See, correct and copy. All your records are visible and editable in the app, and exportable as PDF, CSV or JSON.
  • Delete everything. Settings, Delete All Data permanently erases all entries, budgets, bills and goals stored by the app, and its temporary export files. Files you saved or shared elsewhere stay at those destinations until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes its private data from your phone.
  • Ad data. We hold none. To control what Google holds about your advertising ID, reset it in Android Settings or manage your Google account activity at myaccount.google.com.
  • Your rights and complaints. Because we hold none of your records, you can already see, correct and erase them yourself. For any question, complaint or request under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or other applicable law, write to support@a3sdigitech.com. This address is also our grievance contact; we aim to reply within 30 days.

8. Children

PaisaSet is a general-audience app for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are not child-directed. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the app, delete the app and its data from the phone or contact us.

9. Security

Your records are kept in the app’s private storage and never leave the phone through PaisaSet. The app disables Android cloud backup of its data, hides its screen in the Android recent-apps view (Android 13 and later), and offers an optional App Lock that uses your phone’s own screen lock. The local database is not additionally encrypted (section 1). No method of storage is perfectly secure.

10. Where this app is offered, and legal basis

PaisaSet is published from India for users in India and in other countries where Google Play offers it. The little we process ourselves (your consent choice and any support emails you send us) is handled on the basis of your consent or request. Nothing in this policy is a claim of legal certification under any law.

11. Changes to this policy, and contact

If this policy changes (for example, a new ad format or service), we will update the date above and, for material changes, say so in the app update notes on Google Play. The version shown inside the app (Settings, Privacy Policy & DPDP Notice) covers the same points and works offline.
