Privacy Policy for PaisaSet

Last Updated: October 2026
Developer & Legal Publisher: A3S Digitech
Product Name: PaisaSet
Application ID: com.a3sdigitech.ghar_ka_budget
Contact Email: support@a3sdigitech.com
Applicable Frameworks: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India) & Google Play Store Developer Policy

1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to PaisaSet, an offline-first domestic financial tracking and household budget management application designed, developed, and published by A3S Digitech.

Our core privacy commitment is straightforward:
PaisaSet is built from the ground up to respect your financial sovereignty. All your income records, expense entries, budgets, and savings goals stay strictly on your personal device. A3S Digitech does not operate remote servers for PaisaSet, does not collect personal identity information, and does not sell or share any user data with third parties.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To maintain 100% user anonymity and privacy, PaisaSet operates with Zero Data Collection:

  • No User Identity: We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, physical address, or national identifiers (such as Aadhaar or PAN).
  • No Financial Credentials: We do not connect to your bank accounts, credit cards, UPI IDs, or net-banking services. We never ask for or process account numbers, passwords, PINs, or OTPs.
  • No SMS or Notification Scraping: We do not read your SMS messages, personal communications, or device notifications. All expense and income entries are manually entered by you.
  • No Device Identifiers or Telemetry: We do not access or collect Advertising IDs (GAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, IP addresses, or device serial numbers.
  • No Precise Location: We do not request or track GPS or network location.
  • No Third-Party Analytics Trackers: We have zero integration with third-party analytical SDKs (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, or Mixpanel).

3. How Your Data is Handled and Stored

  • Local Storage in App Sandbox: All transaction records, categories, monthly budgets, and savings goals are stored solely in an isolated SQLite database located in Android's private internal application sandbox (/data/data/com.a3sdigitech.ghar_ka_budget/databases/).
  • Operating System Isolation: On standard Android devices, the Android application sandbox prevents other applications from inspecting, reading, or tampering with PaisaSet's database files.
  • Zero Remote Transmission: PaisaSet does not declare or request the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. The application has no technical capability to transmit data across the internet.

4. Permissions Used by PaisaSet

PaisaSet adheres strictly to the principle of minimal privilege. The application requests only the minimum permissions necessary for local offline utility:

Permission Technical Name Purpose & Scope
Storage Access Framework (SAF) System File Picker (GET_CONTENT / CREATE_DOCUMENT) Used exclusively when you choose to export your records to Excel/PDF or create a local JSON backup. Access is restricted solely to the user-selected file destination.
Notifications (Optional) POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used only to deliver optional local daily budget reminders or recurring expense alerts. All alarms and notifications are scheduled locally on your device via Android's AlarmManager.

5. Data Export, Backup, and User Portability

  • User-Initiated Exports: You can export your financial entries to Excel (.xlsx), PDF (.pdf), or structured JSON (.json) at any time via the Export & Backup screen.
  • Destination Security: Once an export or backup file is created or shared outside the application (e.g., saved to your device Downloads folder, external SD card, or sent via third-party apps like email or WhatsApp), that exported file is subject to the security policies and controls of the selected destination. We advise keeping exported financial files secure.
  • Restore Capability: You may restore your records using a valid PaisaSet JSON backup file on your own device.

6. Data Deletion and Retention

You maintain absolute sovereignty and control over your financial records:

  • In-App Self-Service Wipe: You can immediately purge all transactions, budgets, recurring items, and savings goals at any time by navigating to Settings → Security & Data Management → Delete All Data. This action executes an irreversible SQL truncation of all local database tables.
  • Application Uninstall: Uninstalling PaisaSet from your Android device permanently and automatically deletes the private application sandbox, including the SQLite database and all cached records.
  • Remote Data Retention: Because A3S Digitech does not collect or transmit your records to remote servers, we maintain zero copies of your data.

7. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

  • Zero Advertising: PaisaSet contains no banner ads, interstitial ads, video rewards, or ad network SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads).
  • Zero Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your data to brokers, marketing agencies, financial institutions, or any commercial entities.

8. Children’s Privacy

PaisaSet is a general-purpose domestic budgeting tool and does not solicit, target, or collect personal information from children under the age of 18 (or 13 depending on local jurisdiction). Because no personal information is collected from any user, PaisaSet complies with COPPA and the DPDP Act 2023 requirements regarding minors.

9. Alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

PaisaSet has been engineered in accordance with the key principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • Purpose Limitation & Minimization (Section 8(1)): No personal identifying data is collected or processed.
  • Storage Limitation & Erasure (Section 12): All local data can be erased directly by the user at will.
  • Accountability & Integrity (Section 8(5)): Data is contained exclusively within the user's sovereign Android hardware sandbox.
  • Grievance Redressal Mechanism: We provide clear developer contact information for any inquiries regarding data protection.

10. Google Play Store Data Safety Declaration

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, PaisaSet declares:

  • Data Collected: None (0 data types collected).
  • Data Shared: None (0 data types shared).
  • Encryption in Transit: Not applicable (The app performs zero network transmissions).
  • User Data Deletion Request: Handled locally within the app via Settings → Delete All Data and by uninstalling the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

A3S Digitech may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future software enhancements or statutory amendments. Any changes will be posted to this repository and noted in official Google Play Store application release notes. Since PaisaSet does not collect user contact details, users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
