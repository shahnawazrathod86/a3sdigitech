Aarogya Bestie Privacy Policy

What the Aarogya Bestie app stores, what leaves your phone, and your choices.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Aarogya Bestie (“the app”) is an Android app published by A3S DIGITECH, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India (“we”, “us”). This policy explains, in plain language, what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have. It applies to the Aarogya Bestie Android app only.

In short: your health records, your family’s profiles and the documents you keep in My Family Health stay encrypted on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy, and A3S DIGITECH runs no server that could hold them. Location is used only when you ask for “near me” results. The app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. Our AI helper, AASHU, can use Google Gemini only if you switch it on, and it never sees your saved records. You can lock the app with your phone’s own screen lock, and delete everything the app stores in one tap.

1. What we store on your device

Aarogya Bestie keeps your health records only on this device: blood pressure, blood glucose, vaccination history, pregnancy (ANC/PNC) checkups, TB medicine adherence, menstrual cycle entries, medication reminders, BMI calculations, daily step counts and water-intake entries, saved facilities, AYUSH daily tracker entries, saved AYUSH articles, quiz results, recent searches and the issue reports you create.

My Family Health adds more, all kept only on this device: family member profiles (name, date of birth, blood group, allergies, health conditions, notes, emergency contacts, an optional caregiver’s name and phone number), medicine schedules with the stock you enter and a log of doses you mark taken or skipped, appointments, readings you enter (blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, pulse, temperature, oxygen), the documents you add (prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries, bills and similar files) with their titles, tags, folders and notes, and a history of what you agreed to share.

Every record is encrypted at rest with a 256-bit key that lives only in your device’s secure keystore (Android Keystore), never inside the app’s own data files. Documents are encrypted separately, file by file, with AES-256-GCM. The app does not allow Android or Google to back up this data automatically (Android backups are switched off), so the app never copies it to Google Drive or any other cloud on its own. You can make an encrypted backup file yourself (section 3).

2. What we do not do

This app has no user account, no cloud database and no backend server for your personal or health data. Nothing described in section 1 is uploaded anywhere by this app. The only things that leave your device are described in sections 5, 6 and 7 (and, only when you choose to share, section 3): ads, map and location searches, and, only if you switch it on, the questions you type to the AASHU AI helper. No analytics or tracking SDK runs in this app beyond the advertising SDK described in section 5. We do not sell health information, and we never use it to choose or target ads.

3. My Family Health: profiles, documents, reminders and the app lock

Where it lives. Everything in My Family Health is stored only on your phone. A3S DIGITECH does not run a server for it, receives none of it and cannot see it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it if your phone is lost, reset or the app is deleted, unless you saved an encrypted backup yourself, and we cannot reset a forgotten backup passphrase.

Documents. A document you add (a photo, PDF or other file) is read into the app and encrypted before it is stored, using a separate random key kept in the Android Keystore. The encrypted file sits in the app’s private folder, which other apps cannot read. A readable copy is made only for a moment when you open a document in another app or share it, and it is erased when the app locks, when you leave the documents screen and the next time the app starts. A document that was changed after it was saved is refused rather than opened.

Reading photos on the phone. When you add a photo of a document, the app can read its printed words using a text reader built into the app (Google ML Kit, bundled model). This happens entirely on your phone: the photo and the words are not uploaded. The words are used only to suggest a title, date and tags, which you check before saving, and, if you leave “Make the words searchable” on, they are kept in the same encrypted storage.

Photo location. A camera photo can carry the place where it was taken. When you add a JPEG photo, that location tag and any embedded XMP data are removed before the photo is stored, shared or put in a backup.

App lock. You can lock the app with your phone’s own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password). The check is done by Android on its own screen: the app never sees or stores your PIN, pattern, password or biometric data and only learns whether the check passed. The app stores only whether the lock is on, how long it waits before asking again, and whether it hides in the recent-apps list. While the lock is on, the app can block screenshots. Protected profiles (children’s profiles start protected) ask again before opening. The app lock needs a screen lock to be set on the phone.

Emergency card. A person’s emergency card (name, age, blood group, allergies, health conditions, emergency contacts) can optionally be opened from the lock screen so that someone can help in an emergency. It is off by default and you switch it on per person. Notes, medicines and documents are never shown there.

Sharing and caregiver messages. Nothing leaves the app unless you choose it. When you share a summary or a document, the app first shows exactly what will be shared and asks you to agree, then hands it to the Android share menu, and you pick the app (for example WhatsApp or email). Caregiver messages are written for you and open in your own messaging app; you read them and press send. The app never sends an SMS or any message by itself and has no SMS permission. Each agreement is written to a consent history on your phone, which you can read and clear. Once something is sent through another app, that app and the person who receives it are responsible for it.

Calendar. “Add to calendar” opens your calendar app with the appointment filled in. You save it there. The app does not ask for calendar permission and never reads your calendar.

Backup and restore. You can save an encrypted backup of your family records and documents to a file. Your phone’s screen lock is asked first. You choose a passphrase and the place the file is saved, using the Android save screen (for example your Files app or Google Drive). The file is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM, using a key made from your passphrase, before it is written. A3S DIGITECH does not receive the file or the passphrase, never stores the passphrase, and cannot open the file or reset the passphrase. If you save the file to a cloud service you pick, that service holds an encrypted file under its own terms. Backup files you made are outside the app: delete them yourself when you no longer need them.

If your records cannot be opened. If the key that opens your saved records is lost (for example after a phone reset), the app does not delete anything. It tells you, and you can choose to start again with empty records or restore an encrypted backup.

Not connected to ABHA or ABDM. The app does not link to the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, ABHA numbers or any health-record network. If that is ever added it will be optional and consent-based, and this policy will be updated first.

4. Basic profile (name, sex, age, email)

If you choose to enter your name, sex, age or email address (all optional), it is stored on your device the same encrypted way as your health records, used only to personalise the app (for example greeting you by name), and never transmitted off your device by this app.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Aarogya Bestie shows banner ads on the Home screen, served through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To select, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob can collect your device’s advertising ID, general device information (model, operating system version, language, country), an approximate location derived from your IP address, diagnostics and ad interaction data (impressions and clicks). This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is not stored by Aarogya Bestie. The app declares the Android advertising ID permission for this purpose.

Your health data is never shared with AdMob or any advertiser. Nothing from section 1 or 3 (no health record or document, reminder or profile field) is ever passed to the ad SDK, an ad request or any advertising partner. Ads appear only on the Home screen: never in My Family Health, on the lock screen, in emergency screens, or beside a record or document. The app is for adults only; ads are not aimed at children.

You can limit ad personalisation on your device: Settings, Privacy, Ads (or Google Settings, Ads on older Android versions) lets you reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads. See Google’s Partner Sites policy and Privacy Policy.

6. Location and pincode search

When you search for nearby facilities, your pincode or GPS coordinates and the kind of place you searched for are sent to map-data services solely to return that one search result: Google Maps data (through the Google Gemini API, relayed by a Cloudflare service) and, when Google is unavailable, OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim and Photon search and several public Overpass servers run by independent operators (overpass-api.de, openstreetmap.fr, kumi.systems and private.coffee), plus India Post’s public pincode directory. Coordinates sent to Google are rounded to about 110 metres. The Overpass servers are asked at the same time, so each of them sees your phone’s IP address and the small map area that was searched; Nominatim may receive the coordinates to name the place. To show approximate distances, the address of each result may be looked up by your phone’s own location service, provided by Google Play services. Only the coordinates, pincode or address needed for that search is sent; your name, health data or any other identifying information is never included. The first time Find Care is about to use your location, the app shows a short notice saying so. Location access is requested only when you tap “near me”, never at launch, never in the background, and searching by pincode is always offered as an alternative that needs no location permission. We do not store your location.

7. AASHU, the AI helper, and Google Gemini

AASHU answers health questions in two ways, and always tells you which one wrote each answer:

  • Built-in guide: fixed, checked answers that work entirely inside the app. It sends nothing anywhere.
  • Google Gemini AI: used only if you choose “Use Google Gemini AI” after reading a clear notice. It is off by default, and you can switch it off at any time on the AASHU screen.

When Gemini is on, the question you type and your last few chat messages are sent to Google Gemini so it can write an answer. Before sending, the app masks email addresses, Indian mobile numbers and 12-digit ID-like numbers it finds in the text. Nothing else is sent: never your saved health records, name, age, email or location. Earlier messages that the app answered itself (emergency, crisis and AYUSH questions) are left out of what is sent. The request travels through a Cloudflare relay (section 12) to Google. The relay does not keep your chat questions.

Please do not type personal details (names, phone numbers, medical reports) into the chat. On Google’s free Gemini service, Google may use what is sent to it to improve its products. Google handles that data under Google’s Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms.

Emergency, crisis and other high-risk topics are never sent to Gemini; the app answers those itself with fixed guidance and the 108/112 emergency numbers. Questions about AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa Rigpa, Homoeopathy) are answered only from the reviewed content in the app’s AYUSH Corner and are never sent to Gemini. If Gemini is unavailable or its free limit is reached, the built-in guide answers instead. Chat messages are kept only on your screen and disappear when you leave the chat; the app does not save them.

AASHU is an AI, not a doctor. Its answers, especially those written by Gemini, can contain mistakes. They are general educational information, not a diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional, and call 108 or 112 in an emergency.

Reporting an answer. The flag button under an AASHU answer opens a short form in the app where you choose what was wrong and may add a note. It then opens your own email app, addressed to support@a3sdigitech.com, with your choice, your note, your question and the answer written in, so you can read and change it. The app sends nothing itself; the report reaches us only if you press send in your email app.

8. Notifications and reminders

Medication, vaccination and appointment reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Android’s own notification and alarm system. The app asks for the notification permission when you save your first reminder, and, so that reminders ring at the exact minute, may ask you to allow exact alarms in your phone’s settings. The text is kept generic (for example “You have a health reminder scheduled”) so nothing private shows on a locked screen. No reminder content is sent through, or generated by, any external push-notification server.

9. Step counting (physical activity)

Step counting is optional. If you turn it on, Android asks for the “Physical activity” (activity recognition) permission. Aarogya Bestie then reads your phone’s own step counter to show today’s steps, and estimates distance, calories and active minutes from those steps and, if you entered them, your height and weight. Steps and the water glasses you log are stored only on this device. They are never uploaded, shared with advertisers, or sent to Google Gemini or any other service. Distance and calories are estimates, not medical measurements. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone’s Settings; the app keeps working and you can enter steps by hand.

10. Children and family members

Aarogya Bestie is a general-audience app for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. It lets a parent or guardian keep a child’s health information (growth, vaccination, medicines, appointments, documents) and lets an adult keep records for family members they look after, such as an elderly parent.

Before a child is added, the parent or legal guardian confirms in the app that they are the child’s parent or guardian and agree to the child’s information being kept in the app on that phone. For another adult, the person adding them confirms that the adult knows and agrees. Each confirmation is recorded in the consent history on the phone. A child’s profile is protected by default (it asks for your phone’s screen lock before opening), the child’s information is never used for ads or tracking, and, like all other data, it stays on the phone and is never sent to us. A3S DIGITECH does not collect, monitor or profile any person’s information, including a child’s.

11. Your control over your data, and deleting it

Deleting your data. There is no account to close. You can permanently delete everything the app stores, at any time, from Profile, My data, Delete all my data. Deletion is immediate and on the device: every health record, reminder, profile field, encrypted document and family profile is wiped, the app lock setting is erased, scheduled reminders are cancelled, and the app reopens as if freshly installed. We cannot undo this because we never had a copy. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the phone. You can also remove just one person (and everything kept for them), delete a single document or medicine, clear the consent history, and switch Google Gemini off on the AASHU screen. Because nothing is held on our side, there is nothing for us to delete on request; data held by Google (ads, Gemini, maps) is governed by Google’s own controls and privacy policy, linked above.

Your rights. Because we hold none of your data, you can already see, correct, copy and erase it yourself inside the app. If you have a question, a complaint, or want to exercise a right under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, and nomination), write to support@a3sdigitech.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Your copy of your data. Profile, Privacy & data, Export my data first asks for your phone’s screen lock, then copies your records to the clipboard as plain, readable text. Protected profiles (children) are left out unless you say to include them. Documents, and the words read from them, are never included. The copy is marked private and the app clears the clipboard after about a minute, but another app that can read the clipboard in that time (for example your keyboard) could see it. It is not encrypted: paste it only somewhere private. Backup files and anything you shared are outside the app: delete them yourself.

13. Medical disclaimer

Aarogya Bestie provides general educational information and personal record-keeping tools. It is not a medical device, gives no diagnosis or treatment, and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (108 or 112 in India). See also the Aarogya Bestie Terms of Service.

14. Where this app is offered, and independence

Aarogya Bestie is made for people in India. Its hospitals, government schemes (such as PM-JAY), emergency numbers (108 and 112) and languages (English, Hindi and Gujarati) are Indian, and it is offered on Google Play in India only. Aarogya Bestie is an independent app by A3S DIGITECH. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of India, the National Health Authority (NHA), the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) or Aarogya Setu. Government names appear only to say where a piece of information comes from.

15. Changes to this policy, and contact

If what this app collects or how it is used changes, this policy will be updated first and the “Last updated” date will change. Questions about this policy or your data: support@a3sdigitech.com (A3S DIGITECH, Ahmedabad, India).
