Lumelo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026 · Applies to the Lumelo Android app

Lumelo (“the app”) is developed and published by A3S DIGITECH, under the Lumelo Studio name (“we”, “us”). This policy explains, in plain language for parents and guardians, how the app handles information.

Who this app is for

Lumelo is designed for young children (ages 2–6) to use with a parent or guardian nearby. There is no account creation, login or sign-in of any kind. The app has a single, unnamed child profile stored only on the device.

Information we collect directly

None. The app does not ask for or collect any personal information: no name, email, age, photo, location or contact details. Progress (stars earned, which activities were completed) is stored only in the app’s local storage on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Text-to-speech

The app uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine to read words, letters and stories aloud. This runs entirely on your device; we do not receive any data from this feature.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob to keep Lumelo free. Because this app is directed at children:

  • Ad requests are marked as child-directed and made under Google’s under-the-age-of-consent treatment, which disables personalized and behavioral advertising for this app.
  • Ads only appear between activities (for example after finishing a flashcard deck, quiz or game), never in the middle of an activity, and not on every completion.
  • Ad content is restricted to Google’s “G” (general audiences) content rating.
Google AdMob may still process limited technical data (such as an approximate, non-precise IP address and general device information) as necessary to serve and measure non-personalized ads and to prevent fraud. This is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not control, and do not receive, this data ourselves.

Links to other services

The Settings screen (reachable only after a parent solves a simple math question) may include links to external destinations such as a website or store listing, intended for parents and guardians only. Once you leave the app, that destination’s own privacy policy applies.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

Lumelo is built so that no personal information is ever requested, collected or transmitted by the app itself, and advertising is configured for child-directed treatment as described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, reflect that in the Google Play listing notes.
