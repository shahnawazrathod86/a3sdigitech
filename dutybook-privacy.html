Privacy Policy for DUTYBOOK

Effective Date: October 2026
Application Name: DUTYBOOK
Official Tagline: Every Duty Counts. Every Rupee Matters.
Package Identifier: com.a3sdigitech.kaamwalihisab

Public Policy URL: https://shahnawazrathod86.github.io/kaamwali-hisab/privacy-policy.html

1. Introduction & Overview

DUTYBOOK is an offline-first domestic household management application published by A3S Digitech. The application helps householders record domestic staff attendance (maids, cooks, drivers, babysitters, cleaners), compute accurate wages and advances, track recurring daily vendor deliveries (milk, water jars, laundry), and record payments.

Our privacy philosophy is rooted in total data sovereignty: Your domestic and household records belong to you and stay entirely on your personal device. A3S Digitech does not operate user databases or remote telemetry servers, and never collects, receives, tracks, monetizes, or sells your data.

2. How Your Data Is Handled

Local Storage

All information entered into DUTYBOOK is stored locally on your device in a secure, sandboxed SQLite database allocated exclusively to the application (/data/data/com.a3sdigitech.kaamwalihisab/).

Privacy & Zero Data Collection

A3S Digitech does not receive, collect, transmit, or monitor your locally stored records through the app. There are no advertising trackers, behavioral analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or background network telemetry embedded in DUTYBOOK.

No Account Required

You can use all features of DUTYBOOK without registering an account, providing an email address, creating a profile, or signing in.

3. Data You May Store Locally

The app provides input fields for you to record information necessary for domestic management:

  • Staff & Vendor Profiles: Name, work role/service, optional contact phone number, and agreed wage/unit rate.
  • Attendance Records: Daily attendance status (Present, Half Day, Absent, Paid Leave, Weekly Off, Overtime).
  • Vendor Deliveries: Daily quantities (liters, packets, jars, clothes), unit prices, pauses, and returns.
  • Financial Khata: Cash or UPI payments made, salary advances, and settlement locks.

We do NOT collect or store:

  • Government ID documents (Aadhaar cards, PAN, Voter IDs).
  • Banking credentials, credit/debit card numbers, or UPI PINs.
  • Biometric records (fingerprints or facial scans).
  • Contacts address books or location logs.

4. Device Permissions

DUTYBOOK adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege. The app does not request or require any dangerous Android runtime permissions:

  • ❌ No Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)
  • ❌ No Contacts (READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS)
  • ❌ No Camera or Microphone (CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO)
  • ❌ No Broad Storage Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Any file saving or loading (such as backups and PDF export) relies on the official Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) system picker.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processing

  • User-Initiated Sharing: If you choose to export or share records (such as generating a monthly salary slip PDF, vendor bill, or CSV ledger), the transfer occurs via the standard Android system share sheet.
  • Independent Privacy Practices: Once you share a file with an external service (such as WhatsApp, Gmail, Telegram, or Google Drive), that destination application handles the file under its own terms of service and privacy practices.
  • In-App Safeguards: DUTYBOOK alerts you before dispatching any file to the system share sheet, confirming that the exported document will leave the application's local sandbox.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rules

  • User Control: You retain full autonomy over your records. You can modify, archive, or permanently delete individual staff members, vendors, attendance marks, or payment entries at any time using available in-app features.
  • Clearing App Storage: Clearing DUTYBOOK's storage through your device settings or using the in-app "Clear All Data" feature removes all local database records permanently.
  • Scope of Deletion: Clearing the app's storage permanently removes its local records, but does not delete copies of salary slips, backups, or bills that you previously exported or shared to external destinations or cloud storage.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling DUTYBOOK immediately purges the app's private sandboxed database from your device.

7. Data Security Measures

We employ multi-layered technical measures to safeguard locally stored information:

  • Device-Level Sandboxing: Standard Android process isolation and UID sandboxing prevent other applications from accessing DUTYBOOK's local SQLite database.
  • Authenticated Encrypted Backups: When you generate a manual backup, the database file is encrypted using AES-256 authenticated encryption, with encryption keys derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 using a master password defined solely by you.
  • No Password Escrow: A3S Digitech does not store, know, or escrow your backup password. If you lose your password, the backup file cannot be decrypted.

8. Legal Compliance & Indian DPDP Act, 2023

Under Section 3(c) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, Act No. 22 of 2023), personal data processed by an individual for personal or domestic purposes is exempt from statutory fiduciary obligations.

DUTYBOOK is designed and provided exclusively as a personal domestic tool for Indian households. Because A3S Digitech does not process, receive, or store any user data, A3S Digitech does not act as a Data Fiduciary or Data Processor under the Act.

9. Children's Privacy

DUTYBOOK is intended for adult household managers. The app does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce new features in future versions (such as optional cloud backup or multi-device sync), this policy will be revised accordingly, and users will be provided with clear in-app disclosure and choice prior to any data processing.
