Privacy Policy — I Knowledge 4U

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Overview

I Knowledge 4U ("the app") is developed and published by a3sdigitech ("we", "us"). It is an Islamic learning app covering the Qur'an, Hadith, Prophet stories, Du'as, prayer times, Tasbih, Zakat calculation, flashcards and a Kids Corner. This policy explains what information the app handles. We do not require an account, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Bookmarks, flashcard progress, the Tasbih count, display preferences (theme, larger text) and your selected city are stored only on your device using local storage. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Location (optional)

If you tap "use my location" on the Prayer Times screen, the app asks for Android location permission. Your coordinates are used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and are not sent to us or stored. To show a place name, Android's built-in geocoder may send the coordinates to the device's location service provider (for example Google). You can decline the permission and choose a city from the list instead.

Content loaded from the internet

Some features fetch content online: individual Hadith texts are requested from the open Hadith API served by the jsDelivr CDN, and Qur'an recitation audio is streamed from verses.quran.foundation. These services receive standard connection data such as your IP address and device/network information, as with any web request. No account or identifier from us is attached.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and device and app-usage information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Ads are set to general-audience (G) content, and no ads are shown in the Kids Corner.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads), and learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. The Kids Corner contains educational stories and quizzes for children and contains no advertising, no purchases and no external links. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents who have concerns can contact us using the details below.

Permissions

INTERNET: load Hadith text, stream recitation and show ads. ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: optional, for prayer times and Qibla. The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Security and retention

All network requests use HTTPS. We operate no servers that receive your personal data, so we hold no personal data about you. Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Your choices

You can deny location permission, reset your advertising ID, change ad consent from Settings, or uninstall the app at any time. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data held by third parties such as Google; please use their privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update it in the app and at the policy URL, and change the date below.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact a3sdigitech at support@a3sdigitech.com
