Aarogya Connect

Your Health. Your Services. Your Connect.

A citizen health companion for India. Find hospitals, PHCs, CHCs, pharmacies, blood banks and AB-PMJAY empanelled hospitals near you, keep health reminders, and track readings like BP, glucose and BMI. My Family Health keeps a private profile, medicine reminders and an encrypted document vault for each family member.

  • English, Hindi and Gujarati
  • Health records stay on your phone
  • General information only, not medical advice

Platform: Android (Flutter)

PaisaSet

Har Kharch Ka Hisaab, Har Mahine Bachat.

An offline household budget and expense tracker for Indian families. A Safe Daily Spend view shows how much you can spend each day to last the month, with Cash vs UPI breakdowns, PDF statements and Excel/CSV exports made on the phone.

  • 100% offline, no internet permission
  • English, Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati
  • One-tap Delete All Data

Platform: Android (Flutter)

DutyBook

Every Duty Counts. Every Rupee Matters.

A staff attendance, salary and vendor ledger for Indian households. Track maids, cooks, drivers and other household help, calculate wages and advances, and keep a khata for milk, water and laundry deliveries.

  • Offline, no account needed
  • No ads and no tracking
  • Multiple Indian languages

SocietyDesk

Your Society. One Smart Platform.

A residential society management platform for Indian apartment communities. Residents pay maintenance, raise complaints, read notices and pre-approve visitors; admins run billing and reports; guards get a simple gate screen.

  • Each society's data kept separate
  • Resident, admin, accountant and guard roles
  • Android first

Platform: Android (Flutter) with a cloud backend

NyayaKosh AI

Supreme Court judgments, made searchable.

Source-labelled search over Supreme Court of India judgments, plain-language citizen guides in English and Hindi, and a cross-reference of old and new criminal-law sections. Information only, not legal advice.

  • Search by citation, case number, year or judge
  • Every result clearly labels its source
  • No generated legal answers

Platform: Web app
