Aarogya Bestie Terms of Service

The rules for using the Aarogya Bestie app.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Aarogya Bestie is a family health information and record-keeping app for adults in India, published by A3S DIGITECH, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). By installing or using Aarogya Bestie you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Information only, not medical advice

Aarogya Bestie provides general health information, self-tracking tools and facility-search results from public map data. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and nothing in it is a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Ranges and notes shown beside readings (such as blood pressure, glucose or BMI) are general references, not a diagnosis. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about your health, and never delay or stop care because of something you read in the app.

Emergencies

In an emergency, call 112 (or 108 for an ambulance) straight away. Do not rely on the app, its reminders or its AI helper in an emergency.

AASHU, the AI helper

AASHU answers health questions from a built-in guide and, only if you switch it on, with answers written by Google Gemini, a generative AI model. AI answers can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. They are general education, not advice for your situation. Emergency, crisis and AYUSH questions are answered only from fixed in-app content. Please do not type personal details into the chat. You can report an answer with the flag button under it. See the Privacy Policy for what is sent and when.

AYUSH and traditional medicine

AYUSH Corner describes traditional and complementary systems (Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa Rigpa, Homoeopathy) for general education. It is not a treatment guide. Do not self-prescribe herbs, minerals or medicines, do not stop prescribed medicines on your own, and speak to a registered practitioner or doctor first if you are pregnant, have a long-term condition or are giving anything to a child. Evidence for traditional uses varies and is labelled where known.

Facility and hospital data

Hospital, pharmacy, laboratory and blood-bank listings come from Google Maps data and community-maintained public map data (OpenStreetMap) and, where noted, official AB-PMJAY source lists. Distances are approximate. Opening hours, phone numbers or empanelment status may be out of date; always check with the facility or the official source before relying on it.

Reminders and your records

Medicine names, doses and times are whatever you enter from your doctor's prescription or the label; the app never suggests or checks them. Reminders depend on your phone: battery saving, Do Not Disturb or a blocked notification or alarm permission can stop them, so do not rely on them alone for medicines that matter. Your records are stored only on your phone. If you lose the phone, uninstall the app or delete your data, we cannot recover them. Use Backup and restore to keep an encrypted copy of your own, and keep its passphrase safe, because we cannot recover it. Check what you save.

Other people's information

Only add a person if you are their parent or guardian, or they know and agree. You are responsible for who you share a summary, document or caregiver message with: it leaves the app through the app you pick, and we cannot see it or take it back.

Who may use the app

The app is for people in India aged 18 and over. A parent or guardian may record a child's information, such as vaccination and growth, on their own device. The app is not directed at children.
