Privacy policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy covers this website. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked at the end of this page.

Who we are

This website is run by A3S DIGITECH, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. Contact: a3sdigitech@gmail.com.

What this website collects

This website has no accounts, no cookies of our own, no advertising and no analytics trackers. The contact form does not send anything to us; it opens your own email app with a message you can review and send.

Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. This is handled under Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your name, email address and message only to reply to you. We do not sell or share it, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to access, correct or erase personal data we hold about you, or raise a grievance. Write to a3sdigitech@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

This website is not directed at children. Our children's app Lumelo has its own policy.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
